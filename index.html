Product

A record of every check you can hand to anyone

Screen with proof

Each allow or block decision becomes a signed receipt that names the list version and fingerprint it was checked against.

Two-person clearing

A match can't be cleared by one person. Clearing needs a second, different reviewer, and both approvals are recorded.

Check it offline

The verifier checks every signature and hash link on its own machine. Change one field and the check fails.

Seal it in time

Every hour, the hash of the newest receipt is timestamped through an independent service and anchored in Bitcoin. Only the hash leaves your machine.

How it works

Three steps, all on your machine

  1. 1

    Screen

    Tollgate downloads OFAC's sanctions list and compares each payment address against it locally. The addresses you check stay with you.

  2. 2

    Sign and chain

    Each decision is signed with your key and linked to the receipt before it, with the list version and fingerprint inside.

  3. 3

    Seal and share

    Seal the chain each hour. Hand the receipts to anyone, and they can check them offline without contacting us.

What it doesn't do

  • Matches exact addresses only.
  • Does no name matching.
  • Moves no funds. Your system acts on the decision.
  • Makes no legal determination.
  • Unsealed records can be rewritten by whoever holds the key.
  • The sample key is public, so the sample says nothing about who signed.
