Privacy

Draft, pending legal review. Effective date: November 3, 2026.

This site collects nothing about you.

What we collect

  • Nothing through the site. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. The contact, waitlist and problem-report forms send nothing themselves: they open an email draft in your own email app.
  • Addresses you screen and files you check stay in your browser and are never sent to us.
  • The demo workspace keeps your own actions and your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage, on your device only, so a reload keeps them. It is not a cookie and is never sent anywhere. The Reset demo button erases it.

Email

If you choose to email us, we receive what you send, including your email address. We use it to reply to you. We don't sell it.

The problem-report form adds the page you came from, the last message shown there, your browser and the time to the email draft. It shows you all of it before your email app opens, you can edit the draft, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub receives standard request data such as your IP address, under its own policies. We don't receive those logs.
