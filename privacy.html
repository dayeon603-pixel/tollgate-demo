Privacy

Draft, pending legal review. Effective date: November 3, 2026.

This site collects nothing about you.

What we collect

  • Nothing through the site. There are no forms that send data, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.
  • Addresses you screen and files you check stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

Email

If you choose to email us, we receive what you send, including your email address. We use it to reply to you. We don't sell it.

The "Report a problem" link in the app fills an email draft with error details and your browser's name and version. You can read and edit the draft, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub receives standard request data such as your IP address, under its own policies. We don't receive those logs.
