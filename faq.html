FAQ

What does Tollgate do?
It screens payment addresses against OFAC's sanctions list and gives every decision a signed receipt that names the list version and the time. Anyone with the receipt can check it offline.
Where does it run?
On your own machine. It downloads OFAC's list and compares addresses locally. If you seal receipts, only their hashes go out, to public timestamp services.
Does the demo send the address I type anywhere?
No. The list is a file this page loads, and the match runs in your browser.
Which list does the demo use?
A fixed copy of the digital currency addresses on OFAC's SDN list, published 10/21/2026 and downloaded on 2026-10-27. The app shows its source and fingerprints.
Does ALLOW mean an address is safe?
No. It means that exact address is not on that copy of the list. Tollgate does no name matching and uses no other lists.
Does Tollgate freeze or move funds?
No. Your system acts on the decision.
Who signed the receipts I make in the demo?
A public demo key that anyone can use. A deployment signs with your own key.
What does a passing check prove?
That the receipts are unchanged since they were signed and link up in order. It does not re-run the screening or show the list was complete. Without a seal, the key holder could rewrite the records.

Can I sign in?
